Prestige Teleprompter

Paste your script, prop this on an iPad, and record on your phone. Nothing to install — it runs right here in the browser, and it keeps working with no signal once the page is open.

Getting a natural take
  • Put the iPad directly behind or beside your phone lens — the closer to the lens, the more you look at the viewer instead of reading.
  • Set the speed a touch slower than feels right. Rushing reads as nervous.
  • Look at the camera, not the text, on your first and last line.
  • Shoot vertical for Reels and Stories. Film near a window, window in front of you.
  • Mess up? Pause, breathe, and start that sentence again — it's easier to cut than to fix.
