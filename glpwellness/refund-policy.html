Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026. This draft policy describes cancellation timing, refund handling, pharmacy processing, and support steps for GLP Wellness telehealth weight-loss services.

On this page Scope Eligibility Check Cancellations Provider Review Pharmacy and Shipping Refund Requests Billing Issues Contact

1. Scope

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to GLP Wellness websites, intake workflows, subscription plans, telehealth support, pharmacy coordination, shipping coordination, and related administrative services. Specific purchase terms shown at checkout or in your account may also apply.

This policy is a starting template and should be reviewed against GLP Wellness operations, payment processor rules, pharmacy arrangements, and applicable law.

2. Eligibility Check

The initial eligibility or intake process may be offered without charge unless a fee is clearly presented before submission. Completing an eligibility check does not mean you qualify for treatment, that a provider will prescribe medication, or that medication will be available.

If you do not continue after viewing available plan terms, no paid plan should begin unless you authorize payment or otherwise agree to paid services.

3. Cancellations

You may request cancellation by contacting GLP Wellness at info@glpwellness.com or (469) 457-2876. Cancellation timing matters because clinical review, pharmacy processing, fulfillment, and shipping steps may begin after an order or renewal is submitted.

  • Requests received before the next billing, provider review, or pharmacy processing step may stop future charges or fulfillment.
  • Requests received after provider review, pharmacy processing, or shipping may not cancel that cycle.
  • Cancellation of future services does not automatically create a refund for completed or already initiated services.

4. Provider Review and Clinical Services

Fees associated with provider review, clinical consultation, care coordination, or administrative processing may be non-refundable once those services have started or been completed. A provider may determine that treatment is not appropriate. That clinical decision does not necessarily make completed review or administrative services refundable.

If a payment was collected and no provider review, pharmacy processing, or administrative service has begun, GLP Wellness may issue a refund where appropriate and permitted by the specific purchase terms.

5. Pharmacy Processing, Medication, and Shipping

Once a prescription has been sent to a pharmacy, medication has entered pharmacy processing, or an order has shipped, the related charges may be non-refundable. Medication cannot typically be returned for reuse or resale after dispensing or shipping.

  • Shipping timelines may vary due to provider review, pharmacy processing, weather, carrier delays, address issues, or medication availability.
  • Contact GLP Wellness promptly if a shipment appears damaged, incorrect, missing, delayed, or delivered to the wrong address.
  • Refunds, replacements, credits, or reshipments for shipment issues are handled case by case and may require documentation.

6. Refund Requests

Refund requests should include your name, account email, phone number, order or transaction details, and a brief explanation of the issue. GLP Wellness may request additional information to verify the request and investigate the transaction.

If a refund is approved, processing times depend on the payment method, payment processor, issuing bank, and applicable network rules. Refunds are generally issued to the original payment method unless GLP Wellness approves another method.

7. Billing Issues and Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact GLP Wellness promptly so the issue can be reviewed. Filing a chargeback or payment dispute may delay account support, cancellation handling, or refund review while the payment processor investigates.

GLP Wellness may suspend account access, future fulfillment, or administrative support for unpaid balances, disputed charges, suspected fraud, or payment method failures, subject to applicable law and clinical obligations.
