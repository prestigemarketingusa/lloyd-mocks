Telehealth Consent

Last updated: June 18, 2026. This draft consent explains the nature, benefits, limits, and risks of receiving weight-loss care through telehealth.

On this page Consent Nature of Telehealth Provider Relationship Risks and Limits Prescriptions Patient Responsibilities Privacy Right to Withdraw Contact

1. Consent to Telehealth

By using GLP Wellness telehealth services, you consent to receive care through remote technologies such as online intake forms, secure messaging, video, phone, text, email, electronic records, and other digital tools when appropriate.

You understand that telehealth may be used for weight-management evaluation, provider review, care coordination, prescription decisions when appropriate, follow-up, education, and support.

2. Nature of Telehealth

Telehealth allows a licensed provider to evaluate and communicate with you while you and the provider are in different locations. Telehealth may involve asynchronous review of information you submit, live or recorded communications where permitted, secure messaging, remote monitoring information, pharmacy coordination, and electronic prescribing when appropriate.

  • Telehealth is not the same as an in-person examination.
  • A provider may determine that telehealth is not appropriate for your situation.
  • You may be asked to seek in-person care, urgent care, lab testing, or additional evaluation before treatment can continue.

3. Provider Relationship

A provider-patient relationship is not created merely by visiting the website, reading site content, submitting a general inquiry, or starting an intake. A relationship may be formed only when a licensed provider reviews your information, accepts responsibility for your care, and applicable legal requirements are met.

Providers use professional judgment to decide whether weight-loss treatment, including prescription medication, is clinically appropriate. Not everyone qualifies.

4. Risks, Limits, and No Promised Outcome

Telehealth has potential benefits, including convenience and access to remote provider review, but it also has limitations and risks.

  • Information may be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed, or misunderstood because the provider is not physically present.
  • Technology can fail, including internet outages, platform issues, message delays, device problems, or data transmission errors.
  • Remote care may not identify every medical issue that could be found during an in-person examination.
  • Security safeguards reduce risk but cannot eliminate every privacy or cybersecurity risk.
  • Treatment may cause side effects or may not be appropriate for you.

Weight-loss outcomes and treatment experiences vary. No provider, medication, plan, or service promises a specific result.

5. Prescriptions and Medication

Prescription medication is provided only if a licensed provider determines it is appropriate after reviewing your information. A provider may decline to prescribe, request more information, change treatment, discontinue treatment, or refer you to other care.

  • Read all medication instructions, warnings, and pharmacy materials.
  • Use medications only as prescribed for you.
  • Tell your provider about side effects, medication changes, pregnancy, new diagnoses, surgery plans, hospitalizations, or other relevant changes.
  • Do not share medication with anyone else.

6. Patient Responsibilities

You agree to provide accurate, current, and complete information and to update GLP Wellness and your provider when information changes. You are responsible for maintaining access to the email, phone, device, and internet connection used for telehealth.

  • Respond promptly to provider questions and safety-related messages.
  • Follow provider instructions and seek in-person or emergency care when directed.
  • Use a private location and secure device when sharing health information.
  • Confirm that you are physically located in a state where services are available before each telehealth interaction.

7. Privacy and Communications

Your health information may be used and disclosed as described in the Privacy Policy and Notice of Privacy Practices. Telehealth communications may involve vendors that support intake, messaging, phone, text, email, identity verification, payment, pharmacy coordination, and technology operations.

You authorize GLP Wellness and its associates to contact you using the contact information you provide for care, account, billing, support, and marketing purposes where permitted. Message and data rates may apply.

8. Right to Withdraw or Stop Telehealth

You may decline telehealth services or stop using telehealth services at any time, subject to any consequences of ending treatment or failing to complete recommended follow-up. You may request information about alternative care options, including in-person care.

If you believe you have a medical emergency, call 911 or seek emergency care immediately. GLP Wellness telehealth services are not for emergencies.
