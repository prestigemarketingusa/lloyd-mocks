How GLP-1 medications may help

How GLP-1 medications support weight management.

GLP-1 medications may help support fullness, appetite regulation, and weight-management routines. A licensed provider reviews your intake to decide whether this type of prescription care is appropriate for you.

Reviewed for your profile

Provider review considers your health history, medications, goals, and potential reasons GLP-1 care may not be appropriate.

Guided expectations

Your plan includes practical instructions, follow-up guidance, and a way to message your provider when questions come up.

Discreet fulfillment

If a provider prescribes, fulfillment is coordinated through US-licensed pharmacies and shipped discreetly to your door.

Who it is for

For adults seeking structured weight-management support.

GLP Wellness may be a fit for adults who want a private online path, licensed clinical review, pharmacy fulfillment if prescribed, and ongoing support. Treatment is not right for everyone, and a provider makes the final decision.

Who may not qualify:

  • ✓People whose health history, allergies, current medications, pregnancy status, or other clinical factors make GLP-1 care inappropriate.
  • ✓People in states where GLP Wellness cannot provide telehealth services.
  • ✓People who need in-person care, urgent care, or a different care path.
  • ✓Anyone a licensed provider determines is not a fit after review.
