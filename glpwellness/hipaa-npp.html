HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective date: June 18, 2026. This draft notice describes how protected health information may be used and disclosed and how patients may access this information.

On this page Covered Entity Notice Uses and Disclosures Without Authorization Authorization Patient Rights Our Duties Complaints Contact

1. Covered Entity Notice

This Notice of Privacy Practices describes how GLP Wellness, a Texas LLC, and its affiliated health care providers, when acting as a covered entity or part of an organized health care arrangement under HIPAA, may use and disclose protected health information, also called PHI.

PHI includes information that identifies you and relates to your past, present, or future physical or mental health, health care, or payment for health care. This Notice applies to PHI created or received in connection with GLP Wellness telehealth weight-loss services.

Please review this Notice carefully. This draft must be tailored to GLP Wellness operations before use.

2. Uses and Disclosures for Treatment, Payment, and Health Care Operations

We may use and disclose PHI for treatment, payment, and health care operations without your written authorization, as permitted by HIPAA.

  • Treatment: to provide, coordinate, or manage care, including provider review, care planning, prescribing decisions, pharmacy coordination, and communications with other providers involved in your care.
  • Payment: to obtain payment for services, process transactions, confirm eligibility, manage billing questions, and coordinate with payment processors or other payment-related vendors.
  • Health care operations: to run our health care activities, improve quality, train team members, conduct compliance reviews, manage vendor relationships, maintain records, and support business operations.

3. Other Uses and Disclosures Without Authorization

HIPAA and other laws may allow or require us to use or disclose PHI without your written authorization in additional situations.

  • As required by law, court order, subpoena, discovery request, or other lawful process.
  • For public health, health oversight, audits, investigations, inspections, licensing, and regulatory activities.
  • To report suspected abuse, neglect, domestic violence, adverse events, product issues, or threats to health or safety where permitted or required.
  • For workers' compensation or similar programs, where applicable.
  • For law enforcement, correctional institutions, medical examiners, coroners, funeral directors, or organ and tissue donation organizations where permitted by law.
  • To business associates that perform services for us and agree to safeguard PHI as required by HIPAA.
  • To contact you about appointments, care coordination, treatment alternatives, health-related benefits, products, or services that may be of interest where permitted.

4. Uses Requiring Written Authorization

Uses and disclosures not described in this Notice will generally be made only with your written authorization, unless otherwise permitted or required by law. You may revoke an authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent we have already relied on it.

Most uses and disclosures of psychotherapy notes, uses and disclosures of PHI for certain marketing purposes, and sales of PHI require written authorization when HIPAA requires authorization.

5. Patient Rights

You have rights regarding your PHI, subject to legal limits and verification requirements.

  • Right to inspect and obtain a copy of PHI in a designated record set.
  • Right to request amendment of PHI you believe is incorrect or incomplete.
  • Right to request an accounting of certain disclosures.
  • Right to request restrictions on certain uses or disclosures. We are not required to agree to every request, except as required by law.
  • Right to request confidential communications by a specific method or at a specific address.
  • Right to receive a paper copy of this Notice, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.
  • Right to be notified following a breach of unsecured PHI when notification is required by law.

6. Our Duties

We are required by law to maintain the privacy and security of PHI, provide this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect. We may change the terms of this Notice and make the new Notice effective for all PHI we maintain.

If this Notice changes, the revised Notice will be made available on the GLP Wellness website or through another appropriate method.

7. Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with GLP Wellness using the contact information below. You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
