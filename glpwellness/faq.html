Common questions

Before you begin.

What is GLP-1?

GLP-1 is a hormone pathway involved in appetite and fullness signals. GLP-1 medications are prescription products that may be considered as part of medically supervised weight management after a licensed provider reviews your health profile.

Is GLP-1 weight-management care safe?

Like any prescription medication, GLP-1 medications can have side effects and are not appropriate for everyone. Your provider reviews your intake, health history, and current medications before determining whether treatment may be appropriate.

Do I qualify?

Qualification depends on your online intake, health history, medications, location, and licensed provider review. Completing the intake does not mean a prescription will be provided.

How much does it cost?

GLP Wellness uses transparent monthly plan details shown during the qualification process before you decide whether to continue. Final options may depend on provider review and medication availability.

Who may not qualify?

You may not qualify if a provider determines GLP-1 care is not appropriate based on your health history, allergies, current medications, pregnancy status, location, or need for a different care setting.

How does shipping work?

If prescribed, medication ships discreetly to the address you provide. Timing can vary based on provider review, pharmacy processing, and shipping logistics.

Is telehealth available in every state?

Telehealth availability depends on provider licensing, pharmacy fulfillment, and state requirements. Your location is reviewed during intake before care can move forward.

How does telehealth work?

You complete an online health questionnaire from your phone or computer. A licensed provider reviews your information and, if appropriate, creates a prescription treatment plan with ongoing messaging support.

Can I cancel?

You can contact GLP Wellness for cancellation or account questions. Timing matters for pharmacy processing, so reach out before the next billing or fulfillment step whenever possible.

Is this medical advice?

No. This website provides general information only. This is not medical advice, and prescription products require an online evaluation by a licensed provider; not everyone qualifies.

Have enough context to start?

Start with a private eligibility check. Prescription products require an online evaluation by a licensed provider; not everyone qualifies. Results vary.
