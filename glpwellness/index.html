FAQ

Common questions about GLP Wellness.

Is this legit and safe?

GLP Wellness uses an online evaluation reviewed by licensed healthcare providers. Prescription products are provided only when a provider determines they are appropriate for your health profile.

Who is eligible?

Eligibility depends on your health intake, location, medications, health history, and provider review. Not everyone will qualify.

Is GLP-1 right for me?

A licensed provider makes that determination after reviewing your intake. If GLP-1 care is not appropriate, you will not be prescribed through GLP Wellness.

How fast do I get started?

You can complete the online intake in minutes. Timing after that depends on provider review, required follow-up, and fulfillment if a prescription is issued.

What does it cost?

GLP Wellness uses transparent monthly plans. Your starting price is shown during qualification before you decide whether to continue.

How is my privacy protected?

Your intake is handled through a private online process, and shipments are discreet. GLP Wellness limits information use to care, operations, and required administrative needs.
