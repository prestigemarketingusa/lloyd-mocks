Four steps

A straightforward telehealth process.

Each step is designed to keep care private, practical, and appropriately supervised by licensed providers.

1

Online intake and health questionnaire

Answer questions about your weight-management goals, medical history, medications, and other information a provider needs to review.

2

Licensed provider review

A US-licensed provider reviews your intake and may request additional information before making a treatment decision.

3

Prescription, if appropriate

If the provider determines prescription GLP-1 care is appropriate, your plan and medication instructions are prepared.

4

Discreet shipping and provider messaging

If prescribed, medication ships discreetly to your door and provider messaging remains available throughout care.

Provider review happens before any prescription decision.
You can review next steps before deciding to continue.
Eligible prescriptions are fulfilled through US-licensed pharmacies.
Questions during care can be handled through provider messaging.
Before treatment

The intake is the first step, not a prescription.

Prescription products require an online evaluation by a licensed provider. Not everyone qualifies, and your provider may decide that another path is more appropriate.

What your provider may review

  • ✓Health history and current medications.
  • ✓Weight-management goals and prior care experience.
  • ✓Potential medication considerations or follow-up questions.
  • ✓Whether telehealth care is appropriate for your situation.

Do I qualify?
